# Competition on Kleinanzeigen: services and pricing compared

Basis: four saved competitor ads in `docs/marketing/kleinanzeiger/` (saved 25.10.2026; Puschmann's ad is dated
05.10.2026, WLP's 02.10.2026) and Fluxmedia's own offer (`src/content/de.json` `services.*`, `docs/services-deliverables.md`).
Only what the ads state is used; no outside market data. Four ads are a small sample, so read the conclusions as
indications, not as a market study.

Observations:

- **Nobody offers what Fluxmedia's core is.** None of the four mentions accessibility (BFSG / WCAG 2.2), structured data
  (JSON-LD), crawler access for AI bots, Core Web Vitals measured with a tool, or a documented before / after check.
  Janati comes closest with "KI-/AI-Optimierung", but as a list of wishes without method or deliverable.
- **Prices are rare.** Only two of four show a figure, and both for Google-profile work, not for SEO or websites.
  The two SEO-focused ads hide behind "VB" / "free consultation".
- **Local Google work dominates** (Henrich, Puschmann, partly Janati and WLP). Fluxmedia does not offer Google Business
  Profile or ad management and says so ("Sie suchen Backlink-Pakete oder Anzeigenverwaltung" under "not for").
- **Puschmann has the best ad structure**: itemised package, fixed price, no contract term, optional monthly care, honest
  "no guaranteed position". It is the template to match for clarity.
- **WLP is the weakest on substance** (shouting, "!!!!", unverifiable "1000 projects"), but has the most established name.
- **Legal quality varies**: two ads still cite TMG / RStV. Fluxmedia's block (DDG, Widerruf page, Datenschutz,
  `impressum-option-d.md`) is more complete than all four.

## 4. Where Fluxmedia is weaker

1. **No low-priced entry package.** Puschmann sells a defined 299 € product; Henrich 180 €. Kleinanzeigen buyers compare
   prices at a glance, and "ab 450 €" sits above both while "VB" hides the price on two others.
2. **The offer is wide.** Eight services in one ad dilutes the message; competitors sell one thing each. Answer: one ad with three SEO packages (section 6), other services as an upgrade path.
3. **No mass-market keyword.** Competitors use "Google Unternehmensprofil", "Local SEO", "Google Ads": terms small
   businesses already search for. "GEO / AEO" and "BFSG" are less known.
4. **Newcomer on the platform**: no Kleinanzeigen reviews or "Pro" history yet, while some competitors show years and
   project counts.
5. **Single operator, passive co-partner**: capacity limit for many parallel Kleinanzeigen orders (free checks are
   limited per week for that reason).

## 5. Pricing assessment

- **Hourly rate.** The site says "from 50 € per hour" and that shorter jobs cost more, without a figure. Decision
  (owner's note): show **70 € for jobs under 10 hours per month**, **50 € for larger monthly volume**. This removes the
  low anchor from short jobs and matches what Puschmann's 299 € profile package implies per hour. The site texts must say
  the same before the ad goes live (section 7).
- **Package calculation at 50 €/h** (`services-deliverables.md` uses the same basis; all figures are proposals to
  validate against real hours):

  | Package | Hours | Calculated | Proposed price |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Basic | about 5 h (3 pages 3 h, report 1 h, call 1 h) | 250 € | **250 €** |
  | Standard | 9 h (existing calculation) | 450 € | **450 €** |
  | Komplett | about 19 h (Standard 9 h, accessibility audit 8 h, security check 2 h) | 950 € | **890 €** (bundle saving of about 60 €) |

  Standalone add-ons: accessibility audit from 400 € (existing), security check about 150 € (3 h, proposal).
- **Standard at 450 €** sits above both visible competitor prices but contains much more (5 AI systems, JSON-LD, Core Web
  Vitals, quick wins, re-check). Basic at 250 € meets Puschmann (299 €) and Henrich (180 €) at the price level, with a
  defined deliverable that neither offers.
- **Small-business rule (§ 19 UStG).** Net equals gross, so there is no room to absorb VAT if the threshold is crossed.
  Every price in the ad needs the line "Kein Umsatzsteuerausweis gemäß § 19 UStG", which matters more now that consumers
  are accepted too.
- **Risk.** The insured turnover band is 0 to 30,000 € (policy ends 01.10.2027). A cheaper entry tier raises the number
  of orders, so watch the sum. Report a higher expected turnover to the broker if the channel grows (to-do item 69).
- **Capacity.** One operator. The Komplett package is about 19 h per order; cap the page count (8 pages) and the number
  of parallel orders.

## 6. Recommendation

**Do advertise on Kleinanzeigen: one ad, three packages, a free start, and an upgrade path. Do not use the full service
list and do not compete on the competitors' terms.**

1. **Position as the technical specialist**, not as another "Google ranking" helper: "SEO, KI-Sichtbarkeit &
   Barrierefreiheit, von einem Webentwickler, mit Prüfbericht". That is the uncontested ground (section 3) and matches
   the real strengths (developer, measured, documented).
2. **Three packages side by side** (owner's decision), each with a visible price, an itemised scope and "no ranking
   guarantee", written the way Puschmann itemises his single package:

   | | **Basic** | **Standard** | **Komplett** |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Price (no VAT, § 19 UStG) | 250 € | 450 € | 890 € |
   | Pages analysed | 3 | several (as agreed) | up to 8 |
   | Technical SEO and on-page review | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
   | Core Web Vitals (Lighthouse) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
   | Structured data (JSON-LD) | checked | checked and corrected | checked and corrected |
   | AI visibility test (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI) | – | ✓ dated | ✓ dated |
   | Prioritised measures list | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
   | Implementation of the main measures (quick wins) | – | ✓ | ✓ |
   | Re-check after a few weeks | – | ✓ | ✓ |
   | Accessibility audit (BFSG / WCAG 2.2) | add-on from 400 € | add-on from 400 € | ✓ included (fixes extra) |
   | Security check (passive) | add-on about 150 € | add-on about 150 € | ✓ included |

   Basic is the answer to the 180 to 299 € competitors. Standard is today's offer. Komplett is the upgrade, and the
   bundle is cheaper than buying the parts. Accessibility can also be ordered on its own.
3. **Free start-page check first**: a concrete one-page PDF within 48 hours is stronger than Henrich's feasibility call
   or WLP's consultation. Keep the weekly limit.
4. **Upgrade path in the ad**: close with "Barrierefreiheit (BFSG), Website-Relaunch, Landingpage, KI-Chatbot auf
   Anfrage". Websites (landing page 700 €, relaunch 2,000 €) get their own later ads, not a place in the SEO ad.
5. **Security check, passive only.** Headers, TLS, outdated CMS and plugin versions, exposed files, no active testing, no
   exploitation. Active testing needs written authorisation from the site and hosting owner (§ 202c StGB is the risk) and
   is outside the stated activities. Ask exali (to-do 52) whether it is covered **before** it appears in an ad.
6. **Do not compete** on Google Business Profile, Google Ads, backlinks or "guaranteed rankings". Say what is not offered
   (as the website already does under "not for"), which also screens out unsuitable enquiries.
7. **Hourly rate**: leave it out of the Kleinanzeigen ad and quote per enquiry, or show both tiers honestly (70 € below
   10 hours per month, 50 € above). Do not show a bare "ab 50 €".
8. **Audience: businesses and consumers.** Consumers are accepted, so the ad must contain the full Widerrufsbelehrung
   block and Muster-Widerrufsformular (`widerruf-datenschutz-option-d.md` section 3). The Widerrufsbutton duty applies;
   the site already has the withdrawal page and form. "Nur für Unternehmer" must not appear in the ad.
9. **Trust**: link the references page and the free analysis page, state "Festpreis, kein Abo, keine
   Platzierungsgarantie" (mirrors Puschmann's strength), and keep the insurance information (§ 2 DL-InfoV is already in
   quote, FAQ and AGB).
10. **Measure for 4 to 6 weeks**: enquiries per ad, which package is chosen, share of free checks that become paid,
    hours per order. If hours per order exceed the figures in section 5, raise the price rather than add volume.
